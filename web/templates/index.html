About this app

You have three buckets — 8 L, 5 L, and 3 L — all starting empty. Measure exactly {{ goal }} L in one bucket by filling from an imaginary tap, emptying onto the ground, or pouring between buckets (no water is lost; pouring stops when the source is empty or the destination is full).

How to play

  1. Choose Fill, Empty, or Pour.
  2. Click the bucket to act on (source).
  3. For Pour, click a second bucket as the destination.
  4. Try to win in as few moves as you can. Use New game to reset.
